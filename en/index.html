Privacy Policy — Compresser

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Controller and contact

For the iOS version, the data controller is Maciej Siemiński. For the Google Play version, the controller is LICENCEPRO POLSKA Sp. z o.o., ul. Huculska 6, 00-730 Warszawa, Poland. Contact for both: mail@macsiem.dev.

2. Compression and local data

Compresser compresses images, video, audio and PDF files on your device. Compression itself does not upload your files. The app stores settings, statistics and compression history locally, including file names, types, sizes and dates. Other optional features and services described below can transfer data.

3. Optional backup and export

If account backup is available in your version and you explicitly start a backup or restore, the app uses the selected account: Google Drive app data or the private Apple CloudKit database on iOS. A backup can contain settings, statistics, history and available files referenced in compression history (usually compression results). It is encrypted, but the encryption key is stored in the same account as the backup; this is not end-to-end encryption against the account provider. To delete a cloud backup, use the provider's account storage controls; Google Drive app data is not an ordinary file in My Drive. Apple CloudKit data can be managed in iCloud storage settings. A local export goes to the destination you choose; protect and delete that copy yourself.

Compresser uses data obtained from Google Workspace APIs only to back up or restore data at your request. We do not use this data for advertising, sale, or training AI models. Our use and transfer of information received from Google Workspace APIs comply with Google's user data policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. Optional notifications

If you enable announcements and grant system permission, the app registers an Expo push token and a persistent random device ID with our Cloudflare backend, together with platform, language, app version, premium status and release channel. Registration and activity timestamps may also be stored. Expo and Apple Push Notification service (APNs) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) help deliver notifications. The app refreshes registration while enabled.

You can turn announcements off in the app and change notification permission in device settings. If you are offline, the off request must wait until it can be sent successfully; until then the registration may remain active. After a successful off request, we remove the push token and registration details. We retain a minimal record linked to the random device ID to prevent an old request from registering the device again. Contact us to request deletion. Active registration is kept until the off request succeeds.

5. Diagnostics, advertising and purchases

Sentry receives diagnostic events such as crashes, stack traces, app version and device or operating-system information to help us fix errors. The app filters diagnostic events and redacts free text and exception messages; this filter does not describe every possible transmission by native SDKs. See Sentry's privacy policy for its practices.

Unity Ads provides advertising. Its SDK may process advertising identifiers, device information and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads, subject to device settings and applicable consent choices. See Unity's privacy policy. Apple or Google processes in-app purchases and account/payment information under its own policies; the app uses purchase information to provide or restore entitlements.

6. Purposes, legal bases and recipients

For requested backup and restore, we process data to perform the service you request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). For announcements, we rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)); you can withdraw it by turning them off, without affecting earlier processing. We use diagnostic data on the basis of our legitimate interest in app stability and fixing errors (Article 6(1)(f)); you may object. We process purchase and entitlement information to provide or restore purchases (Article 6(1)(b)). For advertising, consent is the basis where required (Article 6(1)(a)); you can change applicable choices in the app or device settings. Providers include Google, Apple, Cloudflare, Expo, Sentry and Unity for the services described above. International transfers may occur under applicable safeguards.

7. Retention and your choices

Local app data remains until you delete it or clear the app's data, subject to operating-system behavior. Cloud backups and exports can remain after uninstalling and must be managed separately as described above. Active notification registrations remain until the off request succeeds; the minimal record preventing unintended re-registration remains for that purpose. Broadcast logs are initially retained for 90 days; when delivery receipts are checked, the retention period is reset to 30 days after the latest check. Sentry, Unity, Apple and Google apply their own retention practices; see their policies or contact us for questions about data we control.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing, and you may complain to a supervisory authority. These rights can be subject to conditions and exceptions. Contact mail@macsiem.dev. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete cloud copies or third-party records.

8. Security, children and changes

We use safeguards appropriate to the services, but no system is completely secure. The app is not directed to children under 13; contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data. We may update this policy and change the date above when practices change.
